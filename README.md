# Path-Finding-for-UoE
Project title: EdiNav

Prepared by: Yuren, Emese, Oliwier, Gregory

Prepared for: University of Edinburgh

Date: November 25, 2026
_______________________

**Summary and request**

_We are creating an app that not only helps individuals navigate the city of Edinburgh, but is also able to direct students of the University to specific rooms using our algorithm. Navigating new buildings while striving to arrive on time can be stressful. This is why we think it’s important to have a navigation service within the campus. Thus we ask your support to help the universities students._

**Problem and rationale**

The problem we are addressing with this app is the limitation in the current navigation system, which consists navigating within a building, in between floors and rooms. As a first year student myself, I had personal experience trying to find the correct room in a building, especially the first time being there.

**Proposed approach and boundaries**

We will create an algorithm that can map the shortest path between two rooms within a building. Our algorithm will depend on the foundation of nodes and grid which we map the floor plan of the building. The end result will have text instructions paired with 360º shots of checkpoints along the path. For the initial presentation, it is only feasible that we successfully implement this in one building, for it to be extended, in the future, to the whole campus.

**Outputs and success criteria**

Deliverable 1: A prototype of the site for navigation inside Appleton Tower.

Success criterion: Ability to follow the directions successfully from one room to another in the building.
